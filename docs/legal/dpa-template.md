## 1. Subject and duration
Provider processes personal data on behalf of Customer to provide [product name] under the [main agreement / beta agreement] dated [date]. This DPA runs as long as that agreement.

## 2. Roles
Customer is the controller for workspace content (screen frames, transcripts, screen events, Work Maps, teach progress). Provider is the processor. Provider is the controller only for its own account administration and billing data.

## 3. Nature, purpose, data and data subjects
- Purpose: recording work sessions and turning them into Work Maps, voice interviews, teaching.
- Data categories: account email, screen frames of the chosen window, voice audio and transcripts, screen events, Work Maps, usage counters.
- Data subjects: Customer's users and any person visible or audible in a session.
- Special categories: not intended. Customer will not record special category data unless agreed in writing.

## 4. Instructions
Provider processes personal data only on Customer's documented instructions, including this DPA and the settings Customer chooses in the product (retention, redaction, off-the-record phrase). Provider informs Customer if an instruction appears to break the law.

## 5. Confidentiality
Everyone at Provider with access to Customer data is bound by confidentiality.

## 6. Security measures
Provider maintains the measures in Annex 1, at least: per-workspace access control, encryption in transit, encryption at rest by the hosting provider, least-privilege access to production, secrets kept out of code, logging of administrative access.

## 7. Subprocessors
Customer authorises the subprocessors in [subprocessors.md / link]. Provider informs Customer at least [number] days before adding or replacing one; Customer may object on reasonable grounds and, if no solution is found, terminate. Provider binds each subprocessor to obligations equivalent to this DPA.

## 8. International transfers
Data is hosted in [US or EU, to be decided]. Transfers outside Switzerland and the EEA rely on an adequacy decision (including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its Swiss extension) or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses with the Swiss addendum.

## 9. No training on customer data
Provider does not use Customer data to train or improve machine learning models, and uses model vendors only under terms that exclude such training.

## 10. Assistance
Provider helps Customer answer data subject requests, carry out impact assessments and consult authorities, as far as reasonable.

## 11. Personal data breaches
Provider notifies Customer without undue delay, and within [number] hours, after becoming aware of a breach affecting Customer data.

## 12. Deletion at the end

## 13. Audits
