How it works

Train, Map, Teach.

One afternoon of real work becomes a map a new hire can follow, with the expert's reasons attached to every step.

1

Train

The expert works as usual in any app. The agent stays quiet while they type and asks one short question when they pause.

Listening

2

Map

A short spoken debrief closes the gaps. The agent explains the process back, the expert confirms or corrects, and a Work Map comes out.

7 steps · 3 judgment calls · 4 guardrails
3

Teach

A new hire picks the agent. It sits next to their cursor, points at the field it means, and stops them where the expert would have stopped.

The Apprentice Test

Five questions any good apprentice has to answer.

How we decided when the agent talks, what it learns, and when it is ready to teach.

1

When to ask

At natural pauses: after a send, a save, a tab switch. Never while you type. At most one question every two minutes.

Quiet while typing
2

What to ask

The why behind a decision you just made. Guardrails first: limits, exceptions, and when you stop and ask someone.

LimitExceptionStop and ask
3

When it has understood

When it explains the process back in about 45 seconds and you say "yes, that is how it works". Every step needs a score of at least 75%.

Teach-back confirmed
4

Whether the new hire learned

Every step ends as Mastered or Practice next, based on what they actually did on their own screen.

MasteredPractice next
5

Trust

Say "off the record" or press the button and capture stops. Names, emails and bank details are redacted before anything is stored.

Off the record

Off the record, whenever you say it.

Capture stops the moment you say "off the record". Personal data is redacted before storage. Nobody learns from a step until the expert has confirmed it.
