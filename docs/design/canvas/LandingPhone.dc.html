How it works

Train, Map, Teach.

1

Train

The expert works as usual. The agent stays quiet while they type and asks one short question at a pause.

2

Map

A spoken debrief closes the gaps. The agent explains it back, the expert confirms. Out comes a Work Map.

3

Teach

A new hire picks the agent. It sits by their cursor and stops them where the expert would have stopped.

The Apprentice Test

Five questions any good apprentice has to answer.

1 · When to ask

At natural pauses. Never while you type. At most one question every two minutes.

2 · What to ask

The why behind a decision. Guardrails first: limits, exceptions, when to stop and ask.

LimitExceptionStop and ask

3 · When it has understood

It explains the process back and you confirm. Every step scores at least 75%.

4 · Whether the new hire learned

Every step ends as Mastered or Practice next.

MasteredPractice next

5 · Trust

Say "off the record" and capture stops. Personal data is redacted before storage.

Off the record, whenever you say it.

Capture stops the moment you say it. Nobody learns from a step until the expert confirms it.
